# MQ-UX-04 — Top-Level Navigation

**Archive status:** Completed — Strategic Assessment  
**Validation status:** Deferred  
**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Question

Evaluate the proposed navigation:

`Início / Explorar / Aprender / Loja / Sobre / Atendimento`

## Main finding

The primary weakness identified was ambiguity between **Explorar**, **Aprender**, and **Loja**, not the number of top-level items.

A provisional alternative was proposed:

`Início / Inspiração / Guias de compra / Loja / Sobre / Atendimento`

This was not adopted as a final validated architecture.

## Important conclusion

**Atendimento** is appropriate when the destination is a genuine service hub spanning FAQs, order assistance, exchanges, support channels, and continuing pre- and post-purchase help. **Contato** is better suited to a page that primarily provides communication details.

## Decision

Further validation was stopped deliberately because it was consuming resources without materially improving an immediate decision.

The mission may be reopened after meaningful Meta traffic exists and navigation behavior becomes directly measurable.

## Lesson

A strategic assessment can be complete even when deeper validation is deferred. Preserve the reasoning and revisit when the evidence has greater decision value.
