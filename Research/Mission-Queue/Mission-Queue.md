### Meta Program Validation Model

All Meta Intelligence missions and campaign briefs must use this three-stage model:

| Stage | Definition | Current status | Evidence required to advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Hypothesis | **Education creates trust.** The philosophy is Zupp1's guiding hypothesis and operating principle, not yet validated or invalidated. | Active | A coherent system capable of exposing qualified visitors to the hypothesis |
| 2 — Observable Behavior | Qualified visitors demonstrate measurable behavior consistent with greater understanding or trust: reading educational content, deeper exploration, product engagement, appropriate support use, return, purchase, or recommendation. | Not yet tested | Repeated behavioral evidence from instrumented campaigns; controlled comparisons where causality matters |
| 3 — Identity | Customers independently and repeatedly describe Zupp1 using the intended philosophy, for example: “Go to Zupp1 first. They explain things.” | Not reached | Unprompted customer language across cohorts, linked to return, recommendation, preference, or purchase |

**Governing statement:** The philosophy becomes a competitive advantage only after it produces observable customer behavior. Until then, it remains Zupp1's guiding hypothesis.

**System Coherence:** Evaluate the complete conversation created by pages, policies, FAQs, disclosures, support, recommendations, calls to action, educational content, and workflows — not only isolated page elements. The shared principle is: **Toda boa escolha começa com compreensão.**

**Primary external intelligence gap:** Can Zupp1 consistently attract people who value understanding before purchasing rather than visitors motivated only by the lowest price?

**Measurement reference:** [Zupp1 Hypothesis Measurement Framework v1.1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1n140PbGKIlhdf6qiQfmJn_TKQ4OkRXtwwogKEEEx0Pk)
