# Brazilian Home-Goods Ecommerce Market Intelligence: Marketplaces & Decision-Assistance Competitors

*Research compiled August 2026 for Zupp1 market intelligence report. Evidence strength is noted for every claim; conflicting sources are presented side by side rather than resolved arbitrarily.*

---

## Market Context (cross-marketplace)

- Brazilian ecommerce overall: R$235.52 billion revenue, 438.92M orders, 94.23M active buyers in 2025, per ABComm data cited in [Mamba Digital](https://mambadigital.com.br/blog/maiores-marketplaces-brasil/) and [Batedor](https://batedor.com.br/en/blog/estado-ecommerce-brasil-2026).
- Traffic/audience share (Conversion, Jan 2026): Mercado Livre 16%, Shopee 12.5%, Amazon Brasil 9.7% ([Conversion](https://www.conversion.com.br/blog/ranking-ecommerces)).
- BTG Pactual analysis (Mar 2026): combined share of access for Mercado Livre + Amazon + Shopee rose from 76.6% to 82.5% between March 2025 and January 2026. Individual shifts: Mercado Livre 32.3%→33.7%, Amazon 25.5%→26.5%, Shopee 18.8%→22.3%, while Magazine Luiza fell from 13.9% to 11.7% ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/03/trio-de-gigantes-globais-do-e-commerce-esmaga-ainda-mais-plataformas-brasileiras.ghtml)).
- GMV ranking, 2025 estimate (IRTT, via Exame/YAV): Mercado Livre R$138.9bn, Magazine Luiza R$46.1bn, Shopee R$40bn, Amazon R$39bn ([YAV](https://yav.com.br/blog/maiores-empresas-ecommerce-brasil/)).
- Excluding cross-border volumes, Mercado Livre holds roughly 47% of domestic GMV, with Amazon and Magalu around 12% each (2025 estimate, BTG via O Globo) ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/01/os-bilhoes-vendidos-por-shopee-shein-e-temu-no-brasil-em-2025.ghtml)).
- The "móveis e decoração" (furniture/decor) category grew 17% in a recent measured period per a Valor/Globo sponsored report ([Valor/Globo](https://valor.globo.com/patrocinado/dino/noticia/2026/07/16/marcas-brasileiras-ganham-espaco-no-ecommerce-nacional-1.ghtml)).
- Casa & Móveis / construction-adjacent categories represent roughly 11–16% of ecommerce revenue per ABComm/IBGE-derived data ([Panorama do E-commerce report](https://pt.scribd.com/document/932974792/Relatorio-Panorama-Do-E-commerce-No-Brasil-2018-2024)) — evidence strength: moderate, secondary compilation of primary data.

## 1. Amazon.com.br

### Market position
- Amazon Brasil holds roughly 9.7% of ecommerce traffic/audience share per the Conversion ranking (Jan 2026) ([Conversion](https://www.conversion.com.br/blog/ranking-ecommerces)), and 25.5%→26.5% share of access among the top-3 platforms per BTG Pactual (Mar 2025–Jan 2026) ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/03/trio-de-gigantes-globais-do-e-commerce-esmaga-ainda-mais-plataformas-brasileiras.ghtml)).
- Estimated 2025 GMV of roughly R$39 billion, and roughly 12% of domestic GMV excluding cross-border volumes ([YAV](https://yav.com.br/blog/maiores-empresas-ecommerce-brasil/); [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/01/os-bilhoes-vendidos-por-shopee-shein-e-temu-no-brasil-em-2025.ghtml)).
- No furniture/home-category-specific market share figure for Amazon.com.br was found in Brazilian trade press. **Not found** at category level — general marketplace-wide shares above are the best available proxy.
- Amazon's Brazil "Casa" catalog launched in October 2018, covering furniture, appliances, bed/bath, lighting, organization, and decor ([Casa e Jardim / Globo](https://revistacasaejardim.globo.com/Casa-e-Jardim/Publieditorial/amazon/noticia/2018/12/tudo-o-que-amazon-vende-para-sua-casa.html)) — this is dated (2018) but establishes the category's tenure on the platform.

### Decision-support features
- Amazon.com.br carries the "Customer Questions & Answers" (perguntas e respostas) feature standard to Amazon's global platform, per Amazon's official global Q&A guidelines ([Amazon Q&A guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/ask/guidelines/qa/)).
- Amazon Brasil's official community guidelines describe standard reviews, star ratings, helpful-vote mechanisms, idea lists, and reviewer profile pages ([Amazon.com.br community guidelines](https://www.amazon.com.br/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GLHXEX85MENUE4XF)).
- **No evidence found** of a furniture-specific buying guide, size/fit tool, or AI-powered recommendation feature unique to Amazon.com.br's home category (distinct from the standard global Q&A/reviews stack). This is a **notable gap relative to Magazine Luiza and Mercado Livre** (see below), both of which show more visible, category-specific decision-support content.

### Content/SEO strategy
- **No dedicated Amazon.com.br blog or buying-guide content hub for home decor/organization was found** in this research, despite targeted search for "Amazon Brasil blog conteúdo casa decoração" and "Amazon Brasil guia de compras móveis blog." Coverage of Amazon's Brazil home catalog exists only in third-party press (e.g., the 2018 Casa e Jardim feature) rather than owned editorial content. **Flagged as: not found / evidence of absence, not absence of evidence** — Amazon may publish guide content behind category pages not indexed in these searches, but no such content surfaced.
- This suggests Amazon.com.br's SEO strategy for terms like "como escolher sofá" or "melhor guarda-roupa" is comparatively **weak or non-existent** relative to Mercado Livre and Magazine Luiza, both of which have identifiable blog verticals for decor (see their sections below).

### Affiliate program
- "Amazon Associados": official commission for the Casa/Móveis/Construção/Ferramentas/Cozinha category bracket is **8%**, per the official Amazon Associados commission table ([Portal de Associados oficial](https://associados.amazon.com.br/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ)).
- Third-party aggregator sources describe an overall range of 1–15% depending on category ([FluxoPromo](https://fluxopromo.com/blog/afiliado-amazon-como-comecar); [HostGator](https://www.hostgator.com.br/blog/programa-de-afiliados-da-amazon/)) — treat the official 8% figure as authoritative for home goods, these ranges as general context.
- Terms per Amazon's official operating policy: 24-hour cookie attribution window, 89-day cart carryover, R$30 minimum payout, payment via direct deposit only in Brazil, paid roughly 60 days after month-end ([Amazon Associados regulamento oficial](https://associados.amazon.com.br/help/operating/policies/)).
- Note: this is the *affiliate/publisher* commission, distinct from the *marketplace seller* commission. Sellers listing furniture pay 15% up to R$100 and 10% above R$100 per Amazon's official seller guidance for the furniture category ([venda.amazon.com.br](https://venda.amazon.com.br/vender-marketplace/vender-moveis-online)).

### Consumer complaints / Reclame Aqui
- Reclame Aqui (Jan–Jun 2025 window): reputation "Ótimo," average score ~8.7/10, 73,592 complaints, 94.2% response rate, 91.7% resolution rate, 84.9% would-buy-again ([Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/amazon/lista-reclamacoes/?problema=0000000000001454)).
- A more recent snapshot (period roughly Dec 2025–May 2026, per secondary source) shows the reputation slipping to "Ótimo" 8.1, with 120,091 complaints, 87.3% response rate, and 87.1% resolution ([Descontos Top](https://descontostop.com/avaliacoes/o-site-amazon-e-confiavel-comprar-na-amazon-e-seguro/)) — evidence strength: secondary compiler, not the primary Reclame Aqui page for that exact period, but consistent methodology.
- No specific furniture/home-category complaint breakdown for Amazon.com.br was found. **Not found** at category level.

## 2. Shopee Brasil

### Market position
- Shopee holds roughly 12.5% ecommerce traffic/audience share per Conversion (Jan 2026) ([Conversion](https://www.conversion.com.br/blog/ranking-ecommerces)), and its share of access among the top-3 rose from 18.8% to 22.3% between March 2025 and January 2026 per BTG Pactual ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/03/trio-de-gigantes-globais-do-e-commerce-esmaga-ainda-mais-plataformas-brasileiras.ghtml)) — the fastest-growing of the three global players in this window.
- Estimated 2025 GMV of roughly R$40 billion ([YAV](https://yav.com.br/blog/maiores-empresas-ecommerce-brasil/)).
- **No furniture/home-category-specific share figure found for Shopee.** Shopee's home category ("Casa, Móveis e Decoração") is large by SKU count but skews toward small decor items, organization products, and low-ticket goods rather than large furniture, based on category browsing (not independently quantified in trade press) — **flag this inference as unverified/qualitative, not sourced to a specific figure.**

### Decision-support features
- Shopee's product review system requires buyer ratings (1–5 stars) plus optional photo/video evidence, with Shopee Coins incentives for reviews meeting length/media thresholds, per Shopee's official Help Center ([Avaliações do Produto — Shopee Help Center](https://help.shopee.com.br/portal/4/article/77967); [Como escrever uma avaliação — Shopee Help Center](https://help.shopee.com.br/portal/4/article/76378)).
- Reviews can include photo/video from the buyer, and are filterable by "Comentários," "Fotos e/ou vídeo," "Estrelas," and "Produto" per Shopee's own guidance ([Shopee blog on finding product reviews](https://shopee.com.br/blog/avaliacoes-de-produtos-shopee/)).
- **No dedicated buying-guide, size/fit tool, or AI recommendation feature specific to Shopee Brasil's home category was found.** Third-party buyer guidance (not an official Shopee feature) recommends manually checking sales volume, rating thresholds (4.7+), and photo reviews before buying — see [Domicioli's independent Shopee buying guide](https://domicioli.tec.br/blog/como-comprar-bem-na-shopee) — this is *user-generated third-party advice*, not a platform feature, and is presented here as evidence that Shopee itself does not provide strong native decision support, prompting independent creators to fill the gap.

### Content/SEO strategy
- **No dedicated Shopee Brasil blog or buying-guide content hub for home decor/organization was found.** Shopee's own blog content located in this research (e.g., [Shopee blog: Tire as principais dúvidas sobre a Shopee](https://shopee.com.br/blog/descubra-as-principais-duvidas-e-respostas-sobre-a-shopee/)) is focused on platform usage/trust FAQs, not category buying guides. **Flagged as: not found.**

### Affiliate program
- Commission rates cited by third-party aggregators range from 3–20% depending on category, with Casa/Decoração specifically cited at 8–15% ([Achadinhos Pro](https://achadinhopro.com.br/blog/como-ganhar-dinheiro-shopee-afiliados); [FluxoPromo](https://fluxopromo.com/blog/afiliado-shopee-guia-completo-telegram); [Tactus](https://tactus.com.br/comissao-de-afiliado-shopee/)) — evidence strength: secondary/aggregator sources with variable methodology.
- Shopee's official Help Center confirms structural terms: 7-day cart attribution window, 3% commission on live-shopping sales, 3% on Shopee Video-driven sales, and up to an extra 30% commission on designated partner promotions ([Shopee Help Center — comissionamento](https://help.shopee.com.br/portal/10/article/124095-Entenda-o-Comissionamento-da-Shopee)).
- Payment terms: individual affiliates (PF) paid via ShopeePay by the 10th of the month; businesses (PJ) paid via bank transfer by the 30th; R$30 minimum payout ([Shopee blog on affiliate payments](https://shopee.com.br/blog/afiliado-shopee-comissao-pagamentos/)).

### Consumer complaints / Reclame Aqui
- One snapshot shows Shopee at "Ótimo" 8.7/10, with 93,739–93,846 complaints, near-100% response rate, and ~89% resolution ([Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/shopee/)).
- A different period shows "Bom" 7.4/10 with 127,439 complaints and 73.2% resolution (Jul–Dec 2025) ([Descontos Top](https://descontostop.com/avaliacoes/shopee-e-confiavel-e-seguro-para-comprar-e-melhor-ler-isso/)).
- Some analysts caveat that a meaningful share of Shopee's Reclame Aqui complaint volume comes from *affiliates* disputing commissions rather than buyers disputing products, per [TecMundo](https://www.tecmundo.com.br/guia-de-compras/415133-como-comprar-na-shopee-e-encontrar-os-melhores-descontos-e-produtos-mais-procurados.htm) — this caveat is qualitative commentary, not independently quantified, but worth noting when interpreting Shopee's complaint volume relative to peers.
- No furniture/home-specific complaint breakdown found. **Not found** at category level.

## 3. Mercado Livre

### Market position
- Largest platform by both traffic share (16%, Conversion Jan 2026) ([Conversion](https://www.conversion.com.br/blog/ranking-ecommerces)) and GMV (~R$138.9bn in 2025, ~47% of domestic GMV excluding cross-border) ([YAV](https://yav.com.br/blog/maiores-empresas-ecommerce-brasil/); [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/01/os-bilhoes-vendidos-por-shopee-shein-e-temu-no-brasil-em-2025.ghtml)).
- Share of access rose from 32.3% to 33.7% among the top-3 platforms, Mar 2025–Jan 2026, per BTG Pactual ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/03/trio-de-gigantes-globais-do-e-commerce-esmaga-ainda-mais-plataformas-brasileiras.ghtml)).
- Moda, beleza, and casa/decoração are cited as categories growing "above the average" on Mercado Livre per Similarweb 2024 measurements referenced in a third-party seller guide ([Hunter HUB](https://hunterhub.com.br/blog/7-tendencias-mercado-livre-2024/)) — evidence strength: secondary citation of Similarweb data, not the primary Similarweb report itself.
- **No furniture-category-specific market share figure found for Mercado Livre.** Not found at category level (beyond the qualitative "above average growth" note).

### Decision-support features
- Mercado Livre offers a free official tool, "Mercado Livre Tendências," providing daily keyword/product trend rankings by category — this is primarily a *seller* merchandising tool rather than a buyer-facing decision aid, per third-party seller guidance describing the official feature ([Hunter HUB](https://hunterhub.com.br/blog/7-tendencias-mercado-livre-2024/)).
- ML has an AI-assisted auto-answer tool for seller Q&A automation, demonstrated in a third-party video ("Responso" style assistant) ([YouTube demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HDCmopC2H44)) — this is seller-side automation for answering buyer questions faster, not a platform-wide AI recommendation engine for buyers.
- Listing pages carry standard buyer Q&A ("perguntas e respostas") and review/rating sections; ML's own seller guidance stresses detailed descriptions, dimensions, and multiple photo angles for the casa/decoração category specifically ([WooSync seller guide citing ML's own recommendations](https://www.woosync.io/pt/blog/guia-crecer-mercado-hogar-diseno-mercado-libre/)) — this is seller-facing best-practice guidance, evidencing that ML relies on individual sellers to supply decision-relevant content rather than providing a centralized comparison/buying-guide tool itself.
- **No evidence of a centralized comparison tool, size/fit calculator, or AI product-recommendation feature aimed at buyers** was found for the home/furniture category specifically.

### Content/SEO strategy
- Mercado Livre operates an active blog vertical for home content, including: "Tudo sobre móveis e decoração" ([blog hub](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/blog/moveis-e-decoracao/1)), a dedicated "Decoração" tag ([blog decor tag](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/blog/decoracao/1)), a dedicated "Sofá" content tag ([blog sofa tag](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/blog/sofa/1)), a 2025 decor-trends article ("6 tendências de decoração de 2025") ([ML blog trends 2025](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/blog/tendencias-em-decoracao-2025)), and a budget-decorating guide ("Dicas para decorar sem gastar muito," Oct 2024) ([ML blog budget decor](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/blog/dicas-para-decorar-sem-gastar-muito)).
- Category listing pages also surface embedded guide-style content, e.g. "Descubra as melhores mesas baratas e como escolher neste guia do comprador de Mercado Livre" appearing directly within category browse pages ([ML casa-moveis-decoracao listing](https://lista.mercadolivre.com.br/casa-moveis-e-decoracao)).
- This represents **more visible content/SEO investment in home decor topics than Amazon.com.br or Shopee**, positioning Mercado Livre as a moderate content competitor in this specific dimension.

### Affiliate program
- Official commission table (per Canaltech's reporting of Mercado Livre's own published rates) for Casa/Móveis/Decoração: **8% for direct sales, 4% for indirect sales** ([Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/internet/como-se-cadastrar-no-programa-de-afiliados-do-mercado-livre/)).
- Other third-party sources cite a broader 6–16% range depending on methodology ([Hunter HUB](https://hunterhub.com.br/blog/programa-de-afiliados-mercado-livre-como-funciona-comissao-afiliados-ml/); [Mamba Digital](https://mambadigital.com.br/blog/programa-afiliados-mercado-livre/)) — the Canaltech-reported official table should be treated as the more authoritative figure given it cites ML's own published rate card.
- Payment via Mercado Pago, R$30 minimum, paid within up to 60 days, per Mercado Livre's official affiliate terms pages ([ML afiliados oficial](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/l/afiliados-home); [Termos e Condições oficiais](https://www.mercadolivre.com.br/ajuda/30228)).

### Consumer complaints / Reclame Aqui
- One Reclame Aqui snapshot (Jan–Jun 2025) shows "Bom" 7.5/10, with 92,416 complaints and 74.5% resolution ([Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/mercado-livre/)).
- A separate source (Ouvidoria Brasil) shows a markedly worse "não recomendada" 4.5/10 rating, citing "Anúncios e Classificados" as the top complaint category at 28.49% of complaints ([Ouvidoria Brasil](https://ouvidoriabrasil.org/ouvidoria-mercado-livre/)) — dates for this figure are not clearly stated in the source; **flag as conflicting and possibly measuring a different ML business-unit entity or time window than the Reclame Aqui page above.** Present both, do not resolve the discrepancy without a controlled same-period comparison.
- No furniture/home-specific complaint breakdown found for Mercado Livre. **Not found** at category level.

## 4. MadeiraMadeira

### Market position
- CEO-stated figure: roughly **30% market share** of the online furniture category in Brazil, per MadeiraMadeira's CEO as reported by [Exame](https://exame.com/negocios/madeiramadeira-cashback-ipo-vendas-produtos/) and corroborated ("quase 30%") by [Daily Journal](https://dailyjournal.news/news/2026-03-12/madeiramadeira-cresce-208-no-ebitda-em-2025-e-projeta-expansao).
- An older/alternate estimate from a strategy analysis site puts MadeiraMadeira at 18% share vs. 12% for competitor Mobly ([NextSprints](https://nextsprints.com/guide/madeiramadeira-product-strategy-guide)) — **flag as a conflicting, likely outdated estimate**; the company's own CEO statement (~30%) is more current and should be weighted more heavily, though it is also self-reported.
- Ranked #3 furniture ecommerce store in Brazil by 2024 revenue (US$120.2M, representing 36.5% of the top-3 combined share) per ECDB's ecommerce database ranking ([ECDB](https://ecommercedb.com/ranking/stores/br/furniture)).
- MadeiraMadeira reported 20.8% EBITDA growth in 2025 with continued expansion plans, per [Daily Journal](https://dailyjournal.news/news/2026-03-12/madeiramadeira-cresce-208-no-ebitda-em-2025-e-projeta-expansao).

### Decision-support features
- Operates a dedicated content hub, "Central de Dicas" (blog), covering home/decor/lifestyle topics ([madeiramadeira.com.br/central-de-dicas](https://www.madeiramadeira.com.br/central-de-dicas)), including specific buying-guide articles such as "Melhores guarda-roupas de 2024" ([MadeiraMadeira wardrobe guide](https://www.madeiramadeira.com.br/central-de-dicas/artigos/melhores-guarda-roupas)).
- Category pages embed measurement/space-planning guidance directly for furniture and decor categories (per direct review of madeiramadeira.com.br moveis/decoracao category pages).
- Operates a physical "Guide Shop" showroom retail format that lets customers see and touch furniture before buying online, per company social media (Instagram/YouTube) — evidence strength: company-owned social channels, moderate.
- This content hub plus physical showroom concept represents the **most developed decision-support content strategy among the five marketplaces for the furniture category specifically** — MadeiraMadeira is furniture-specialized, unlike the four horizontal marketplaces.

### Content/SEO strategy
- The "Central de Dicas" content hub functions as MadeiraMadeira's SEO play for home-decor-related search terms, directly targeting buying-guide-style queries (e.g., "melhores guarda-roupas") ([Central de Dicas](https://www.madeiramadeira.com.br/central-de-dicas); [wardrobe guide example](https://www.madeiramadeira.com.br/central-de-dicas/artigos/melhores-guarda-roupas)).
- No independent SEO ranking/traffic data (e.g., Similarweb keyword rankings) was found to quantify how well this content actually ranks for target terms. **Flag as: content strategy confirmed to exist; SEO performance/ranking strength not independently verified.**

### Affiliate program
- Runs an affiliate program via the Awin network with a cited commission of approximately 3.5%, per an independent YouTube analysis of the program ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l8EfSUQnQDo)) — this source also states the program requires a CNPJ (business registration), i.e., is not open to individual (PF) affiliates.
- Also listed on the Lomadee affiliate network with a CPA (cost-per-acquisition) model and a 30-day cookie window ([Lomadee](https://www.lomadee.com.br/pt-br/marcas/madeiramadeira)).
- Distinct from the affiliate program, MadeiraMadeira's **marketplace seller** commission table charges 19% for Móveis/Decoração/Móveis Estofados, varying 13.8%–19.6% by subcategory, per the company's own seller policy documentation ([MadeiraMadeira Zendesk seller policy](https://madeiramadeira.zendesk.com/hc/pt-br/articles/9485012209037-Pol%C3%ADticas-de-Comissionamento)).

### Consumer complaints / Reclame Aqui
- Official Reclame Aqui page (period 01/01/2025–30/06/2025): reputation "Bom," average score **7.5/10**, 12,796 complaints received, 100% response rate, 85.1% resolution rate, 57.4% would-buy-again, average response time 6 days 14 hours ([Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/madeiramadeira/)).
- Multiple secondary sources corroborate a similar range (7.2–8.1) across different measurement windows: one shows "ótima" 8.5/10 with 5,708 complaints, 98.8% response, and 93.9% resolution, citing "móveis em geral" (65.68%) as the top complaint category followed by "móveis" (38.54%) and delivery delays (22.91%) ([Ouvidoria Brasil](https://ouvidoriabrasil.org/ouvidoria-madeiramadeira/)); another shows a historical range of 7.3–8.1 with 98–99.5% response rates ([Adoro Cupom](https://adorocupom.com/blog/como-saber-se-a-madeira-madeira-e-confiavel/)).
- This is the **only one of the five marketplaces with an explicit, sourced complaint-category breakdown** ("móveis em geral," "móveis," "atraso na entrega") directly relevant to the furniture vertical — valuable evidence for Zupp1's positioning, since delivery delays and general furniture-quality issues are named as leading complaint drivers.
- Individual complaint narratives on Reclame Aqui (anecdotal, not statistically representative) include delivery/product-quality disputes, e.g. ["MADEIRA MADEIRA É UM PESADELO NA MINHA VIDA"](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/madeiramadeira/madeira-madeira-e-um-pesadelo-na-minha-vida_dkUHuK_LdS4-k1mA/) and ["MADEIRA MARKETPLACE. G0LPE E ENGANAÇÃO"](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/madeiramadeira/madeira-marketplace-g0lpe-e-enganacao_bp5CAaDZyAtEAWob/) — cited here only as illustrative of complaint tone/type, not as statistical evidence.

## 5. Magazine Luiza (Magalu)

### Market position
- GMV of roughly R$46.1 billion in 2025 (IRTT estimate via YAV/Exame) ([YAV](https://yav.com.br/blog/maiores-empresas-ecommerce-brasil/)), and roughly 12% of domestic GMV excluding cross-border ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/01/os-bilhoes-vendidos-por-shopee-shein-e-temu-no-brasil-em-2025.ghtml)).
- Traffic/access share fell from 13.9% to 11.7% between March 2025 and January 2026 per BTG Pactual — the only one of the major platforms losing share in this window, as the three global players (ML, Amazon, Shopee) gained ground ([O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/blogs/capital/post/2026/03/trio-de-gigantes-globais-do-e-commerce-esmaga-ainda-mais-plataformas-brasileiras.ghtml)).
- **No furniture-category-specific market share figure found for Magazine Luiza.** Not found at category level.

### Decision-support features
- **"Lu" virtual influencer AI**: generative AI feature launched July 2023 (built on Luizalabs + Google Vertex AI) offering personalized product recommendations based on stated buyer needs (e.g., "best smartphone for battery life") — confirmed via Magazine Luiza's own investor-relations materials ([Magazine Luiza IR PDF](https://ri.magazineluiza.com.br/Download.aspx?Arquivo=F+gcCuq0PbMeHFIzyzqDKA==)) and corroborated by [Estadão](https://www.estadao.com.br/economia/negocios/cerebro-da-lu-magazine-luiza-aplica-inteligencia-artificial-para-melhorar-interacao-com-consumidor/) and [IT Forum](https://itforum.com.br/noticias/ia-generativa-magalu-dasa-google/).
- **"WhatsApp da Lu" — AI Commerce**, launched November 5, 2025: a full conversational shopping journey (search, personalized recommendation, payment via Pix/card, post-sale support) inside WhatsApp, built in partnership with Meta, Google, and Bain & Company, using a multi-agent AI architecture. Initially rolled out to 1 million recurring customers, with plans to scale to over 30 million active consumers by end of 2025; covers Magalu's 1P catalog plus 300,000+ marketplace sellers and 37 million+ listings — per official Magazine Luiza investor materials ([Magalu IR PDF, Nov 2025](https://ri.magazineluiza.com.br/List/Download.aspx?Arquivo=8lqkxA0eHuthoyoNG0YM0w==)) and corroborated by [O Globo](https://oglobo.globo.com/economia/negocios/noticia/2025/11/05/ai-commerce-magalu-comeca-a-operar-ferramenta-de-compras-com-inteligencia-artificial-veja-como-funciona.ghtml).
- A June 2026 follow-up investor release states WhatsApp da Lu generated over 9 million conversations in its first 6 months, with 3x the conversion rate of other channels ([Magalu IR PDF, June 2026](https://ri.magazineluiza.com.br/List/Download.aspx?Arquivo=8lqkxA0eHuthoyoNG0YM0w==)).
- Furniture category pages carry structured, embedded buying-guide-style content directly on-page — e.g., the sofa category page includes Q&A-style sections such as "Qual a melhor marca de sofá" and "Qual tamanho de sofá escolher," plus filter, comparison, and review-sorting tools ([magazineluiza.com.br/sofas](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/sofas/moveis/s/mo/msof/)).
- **This is the strongest AI-powered decision-support feature set found across all five marketplaces** — Magalu is the only platform in this research with a documented, company-confirmed generative-AI shopping assistant operating at meaningful scale (millions of conversations) specifically for personalized recommendations and end-to-end conversational commerce.

### Content/SEO strategy
- Operates "Blog da Lu," a substantial content hub with a dedicated "Casa e Construção" vertical ([Blog da Lu — Casa e Construção](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/blog-da-lu/l/cj/casa-e-construcao/31/)), including articles like "Dicas de decoração para a sua casa nova" (May 2025) ([Blog da Lu decor article](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/blog-da-lu/c/ud/ordu/dicas-de-decoracao-para-a-sua-casa-nova/13513/)) and a "Casa Inteligente" (smart home) content vertical updated as recently as March 2026 ([Blog da Lu — Casa Inteligente](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/blog-da-lu/l/ci/casa-inteligente/2/)).
- Also operates "Mundo Casa Magalu," a dedicated special-content hub for home, furniture, and decor ([especiais.magazineluiza.com.br/mundo-casa](https://especiais.magazineluiza.com.br/mundo-casa/)).
- Category pages themselves (not just the blog) embed decor/buying-guide content, as noted above for the sofa category.
- This combination (dedicated blog vertical + smart-home content + embedded category-page guides) represents a **broad, multi-format content/SEO strategy for home decor**, comparable in scope to Mercado Livre's blog effort and exceeding Amazon.com.br's and Shopee's (neither of which showed comparable content).

### Affiliate program
- "Parceiro Magalu" / "Influenciador Magalu" (formerly branded "Magazine Você"): commission cited at 1–12%, with up to 12% for cama/mesa (bed/table linens) at the top "Diamante" tier, per third-party analysis ([Doug Empreendimentos](https://douginformatica.com.br/blog/programa-de-afiliados-magazine-luiza-parceiro-magalu-como-funciona-e-lucre-ate-12); [Afily](https://afily.com.br/magalu/)) and the official program site ([Influenciador Magalu — parceiromagalu.com.br](https://www.parceiromagalu.com.br/divulgador)).
- R$50 minimum payout, paid within up to 34 days. Tier structure: Bronze (1,000 pts), Prata (1,500 pts), Ouro (3,500 pts), Diamante (8,000 pts) per the sources above.

### Consumer complaints / Reclame Aqui
- Reclame Aqui reputation: RA1000 status, average score in the 8.3–8.4/10 range depending on measurement period, with complaint counts ranging roughly 58,740–79,776 depending on the window, and resolution rates above 90% across multiple cited snapshots ([Reclame Aqui](https://www.reclameaqui.com.br/empresa/magazine-luiza-loja-online/); [EConfiável](https://ehconfiavel.com.br/blog/a_magazine_luiza_e_confiavel_um_panorama_sobre_a_excelencia_no_atendimento_e_a_experiencia_digital)).
- Magazine Luiza won "Loja Online," "Loja Física," and "Cartões de Crédito" category awards at the Prêmio Reclame Aqui 2025 ([EConfiável](https://ehconfiavel.com.br/blog/a_magazine_luiza_e_confiavel_um_panorama_sobre_a_excelencia_no_atendimento_e_a_experiencia_digital)).
- In contrast, Trustpilot (an international review platform, not Brazil-specific) shows a markedly worse 1.8/5 rating from 362 reviews ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/magazineluiza.com.br)) — **flag as conflicting evidence from a platform with a much smaller, likely self-selected and internationally-skewed review base**; Reclame Aqui, being the Brazil-specific and far higher-volume platform, should be weighted more heavily for a Brazilian market intelligence purpose.
- No furniture-specific complaint breakdown found for Magazine Luiza. **Not found** at category level.

## 6. Decision-Assistance / Comparison Platform Competitors (Home & Furniture Category)

This section covers Brazilian sites that function as dedicated comparison, curation, or buying-guide platforms for home/furniture/decor — the direct or indirect competitive set for a "decision-assistance platform" positioning.

### Ordenato
- Started in 2015 as a décor-focused blog and evolved into what it calls a "guia de e-commerce" (ecommerce guide), curating home/décor/organization product recommendations and linking out to marketplaces including Amazon, Shopee, and Mercado Livre, per its own "Quem Somos" page ([Ordenato — Quem Somos](https://ordenato.com.br/quem-somos/)).
- This is the **closest direct analog found to a curation/decision-assistance affiliate model** for home goods specifically spanning multiple marketplaces. Evidence strength: primary source (company's own about page), but no independent traffic/scale data (e.g., Similarweb) was found to size Ordenato's actual reach or market impact. **Scale: not found/not verified.**

### Westwing Brasil
- A curated home-decor retailer (not a pure comparison site) that embeds detailed buying-guide content directly into its commercial category pages — e.g., a sofa-buying guide with comparison tables, room-size matching guidance, and technical specifications ([Westwing sofa guide](https://www.westwing.com.br/sala-de-estar/sofas/)).
- Functions as a **hybrid retailer-plus-content model**: Westwing sells its own curated catalog while using buying-guide content as a merchandising/SEO tool, rather than operating as an independent, marketplace-agnostic decision-assistance layer. Relevant as a benchmark for content depth and format, less relevant as a direct competitor to a cross-marketplace decision-assistance concept since Westwing is itself a retailer with its own inventory.

### SofasExpres.com and Sofá Review
- Both are dedicated, single-category (sofa) comparison/review sites offering price comparison across multiple stores and "como escolher" (how-to-choose) buying-guide content ([SofasExpres](https://sofasexpres.com/); [Sofá Review](https://sofareview.com.br/)).
- These demonstrate that **vertical-specific comparison sites already exist in the Brazilian market for at least one furniture subcategory (sofas)** — direct evidence of unmet/addressed demand for decision assistance in furniture, and a template for what a broader multi-category home-goods decision-assistance platform (Zupp1's positioning) could look like if expanded across product types.
- No independent traffic/scale/revenue data found for either site. **Scale: not found.**

### Mybest Brazil
- A general product review/ranking site (a Brazilian localization of the international "my-best" ranking format) with dedicated "melhores X" (best-of) ranking articles for guarda-roupas (wardrobes) and sofás, using a stated methodology combining Reclame Aqui scores, Amazon review scores, and general popularity signals ([Mybest guarda-roupas ranking](https://br.my-best.com/18473); [Mybest sofás ranking](https://br.my-best.com/20849)).
- This is a **cross-category comparison/ranking site and one of the more direct competitor analogs found** — it explicitly aggregates third-party trust signals (Reclame Aqui + marketplace reviews) into a synthesized ranking, which is conceptually close to a "decision-assistance" value proposition. No independent traffic data found to size its actual market reach. **Scale: not found.**

### JáCotei
- An AI-powered price-comparison engine allowing users to search by text, photo, or audio, and covering furniture categories including "Móveis: Sofá, Cama, Guarda-Roupa e mais" ([JáCotei móveis category](https://jacotei.com.br/moveis); [JáCotei homepage](https://jacotei.com.br/)).
- Positioned as a general price comparator with AI search tooling rather than a decor-specific curation/buying-guide platform — relevant primarily as a **tooling benchmark** (AI-powered search/comparison UX) rather than a content-driven decision-assistance competitor. Has a mobile app with price-history and price-alert features per its Google Play listing ([JáCotei on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=pl&id=br.com.jacotei.aplicativo)).

### Méliuz
- A major Brazilian cashback company that also publishes buying-guide blog content for furniture, including a guarda-roupa (wardrobe) comparison article with a price table across retailers ([Méliuz guarda-roupas guide](https://www.meliuz.com.br/blog/melhores-guarda-roupas/)).
- Worth flagging as an **adjacent competitor combining a cashback business model with buying-guide content** — a different monetization mechanism than pure affiliate curation, but serving a similar "help the buyer decide, then route them to a marketplace" function.

### Mazco Decor
- Describes itself as "curadoria brasileira para casas com identidade" (Brazilian curation for homes with identity) and operates primarily through WhatsApp-based customer contact ([Mazco Decor](https://mazco.com.br/)) — appears to be a **smaller, boutique curation service** rather than a scaled decision-assistance platform. No independent scale/traffic data found. **Scale: not found** — treat as a minor/niche player pending further verification.

### Other smaller/niche players noted (lower confidence, lower relevance)
- Criatto Studio — an interior-design curation service, not fundamentally an ecommerce decision-assistance platform ([Criatto Studio](https://criattostudio.com.br/servicos/curadoria.html)).
- Província — publishes furniture buying-guide-style blog content (e.g., "Os 10 guarda-roupas mais populares do momento") but appears to be a smaller blog/media property rather than a platform with its own comparison infrastructure ([Província blog](https://provincia.casa/blogs/magazine/os-10-guarda-roupas-mais-populares-do-momento)).
- "Uma Casa Para Decorar" — an independent decor content creator/blogger with e-books and an Amazon storefront, referenced via a YouTube video description ([YouTube video referencing Uma Casa Para Decorar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=en_ehbT2V4s)) — illustrates that independent creator-led decor content and affiliate monetization already exists in this space at the individual-creator scale, alongside company-run platforms.

### Summary assessment for Zupp1
- **No large-scale, well-funded, dedicated "decision-assistance platform" spanning all of furniture + home organization + decor + comparison-across-marketplaces was identified in this research.** The closest analogs are: (a) single-category comparison sites (SofasExpres, Sofá Review) proving demand exists but only for one subcategory; (b) Mybest Brazil, a cross-category ranking aggregator using trust-signal synthesis; and (c) Ordenato, a multi-marketplace curation blog. None of these were confirmed (via independent traffic/scale data) to operate at a scale comparable to the major marketplaces themselves.
- This suggests a **plausible white-space opportunity** for a purpose-built, multi-category home-goods decision-assistance platform in Brazil — but this assessment is qualitative and based on the absence of a clear large-scale incumbent in available sources, not a definitive competitive-landscape claim. Independent traffic/market-sizing data for each candidate competitor would be needed to fully validate this conclusion, and none was found for any of the dedicated comparison sites identified.
