# USS ZUPP1 — XO-004 Biblioteca Complete Editorial Audit

**Status:** Read-only published-article audit completed; draft publication gate completed  
**Audit date:** 11 August 2026  
**Scope:** 55 published articles and 21 drafts  
**Implementation:** No published article modified. No draft published. All 21 rejected drafts moved to recoverable WordPress Trash by Captain's order.

## Executive finding

The Biblioteca has a strong structural foundation: all 55 published articles use the approved `.zupp-post` container, contain one H1, at least one image, a WhatsApp continuity block and a “Continue exploring” area. The newer Draft Engine output (IDs 4257–4271) is materially stronger, more complete and less promotional than much of the July catalog.

The primary weakness is not layout. It is editorial and commercial consistency. Only 24 of 55 articles have custom AIOSEO titles and descriptions; 14 are under 500 words; 186 affiliate links are distributed across the catalog; several older articles contain 7–11 affiliate destinations; one article (ID 2133) has six affiliate links without a disclosure. Several high-risk pet, cleaning and DIY articles contain irrelevant products or claims that need expert/label verification.

## System-level recommendations

1. Define the plugin formally as the **Biblioteca Draft Engine**. Generation creates a candidate; it does not confer publication readiness.
2. Add a pre-publication gate: duplicate-intent check, factual-risk classification, human editorial pass, product relevance check, link validation, disclosure check, SEO uniqueness check and rendered-page check.
3. Replace product-volume targets with a maximum of one or two earned recommendations near the conclusion.
4. Create the standard four-lane “Continue Exploring” component requested by XO-004.
5. Prioritize veterinary, cleaning-chemical and DIY/tool articles before stylistic work.
6. Resolve search-intent overlap before expanding thin articles; otherwise expansion may worsen cannibalization.

## Draft Engine policy adopted for implementation planning

- **Role:** Generate high-quality first drafts only.
- **Required human gates:** intent uniqueness, editorial voice, technical evidence, reader next step, product relevance, affiliate disclosure, metadata, and final rendered-page QA.
- **Automatic stop conditions:** duplicate topic, medical/veterinary claim, chemical-safety claim, electrical/structural/tool risk, unverified product facts, missing source, missing disclosure, or non-standard HTML.
- **PD-001:** AI accelerates drafting; it does not eliminate editorial responsibility.
- **PD-002:** The article succeeds when the reader is more capable of making a good decision, not merely when it ranks.
- **PD-003:** Education before recommendation; understanding before purchase; trust before conversion.
