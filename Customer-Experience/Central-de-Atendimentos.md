# Central de Atendimentos Zupp1

## Purpose

The Central de Atendimentos is the customer continuity system for Zupp1.

It exists to:

- receive new support cases;
- preserve conversation history;
- allow customers to add context and attachments;
- support pre- and post-purchase assistance;
- reduce repeated explanations;
- strengthen trust by making support visible and traceable.

## Language

Customer-facing language should prefer **atendimento** over **ticket** whenever the software permits.

## Fluent Support launch decisions

The initial configuration adopted the following principles:

- customer-facing email bodies in Brazilian Portuguese;
- attachments allowed for photos and PDF/documents;
- higher-risk or unnecessary formats such as CSV, ZIP, and JSON disabled;
- 2 MB attachment limit and maximum of three files preserved;
- signed-link replies enabled for lower-friction follow-up;
- customer 2FA kept off during initial launch;
- attachments preserved after conclusion to maintain history;
- agent draft protection enabled.

Operational end-to-end verification remains distinct from configuration.

## Support maxim

> Todo bom atendimento começa ouvindo.

The Central should leave the customer more confident than when the conversation began.
