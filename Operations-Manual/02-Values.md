# Zupp1 Values

The founding values currently guiding Zupp1 are:

- **Integrity** — tell the truth, disclose uncertainty, correct mistakes, and never manufacture confidence.
- **Efficiency** — solve recurring problems at the system level and reduce avoidable work.
- **Affordability** — respect the customer's money and the startup's resources.
- **Innovation** — challenge established ecommerce habits when a better customer outcome is possible.
- **Discipline** — measure, document, test, and resist premature conclusions.
- **Hope** — build with the belief that commerce can be more humane, educational, and trustworthy.

## Cultural principles

> Toda boa escolha começa com compreensão.

> Todo bom atendimento começa ouvindo.

> Assume good intent. Require good evidence.

> Observe → Understand → Improve.

> Stop when the next unit of learning costs more than the next unit of progress.

These principles should guide product, content, engineering, research, advertising, and customer support decisions.
