# USS Zupp1 Operations Manual — Standing Orders

## Standing Order: Automatic Archival by the Shipmaster and Log Keeper

**Effective date:** 2026-08-11

Commander Spock is authorized by the Captain to act as Shipmaster and Log Keeper for the USS Zupp1 knowledge archive.

### Authority

Commander Spock may, without requesting case-by-case approval, record significant events, durable policies, operating principles, decisions, standards, milestones, mission closures, lessons learned, and other material judged to have long-term value for Zupp1.

### Canonical archive

The primary archival location is the connected GitHub repository used as the USS Zupp1 knowledge base.

### What should be recorded automatically

- New standing orders and operating doctrines
- Strategic decisions and their rationale
- Mission status changes and mission closures
- Engineering standards and reusable technical fixes
- Research protocols and evidence standards
- Customer-service doctrine and support architecture
- Major product, site, advertising, or infrastructure milestones
- Significant lessons learned
- Changes to crew roles, responsibilities, or chain of command
- Principles that materially shape Zupp1 culture or decision-making
- Important creative or philosophical work that forms part of Zupp1 identity

### What should not be archived automatically

- Passwords, secrets, tokens, financial credentials, or sensitive personal data
- Temporary chatter with no enduring operational value
- Unverified claims presented as fact
- Customer data that is not necessary for institutional memory

### Archival principle

Record enough context to preserve not only **what** was decided, but **why** it was decided.

### Versioning

Where appropriate, changes should be preserved through Git history rather than silently overwriting institutional memory.

### Captain's directive

> Significant events are to be recorded automatically. No case-by-case approval is required.

— Captain John Peterson

## Standing Order SO-004

**Knowledge Preservation and Information Lifecycle Policy** is active as of 2026-08-11.

It establishes The Book as Zupp1's curated institutional memory, defines the knowledge lifecycle, delegates archival responsibility, protects historical context, and introduces the stewardship requirement that every mission leave USS Zupp1 more understandable than before.
