# USS Zupp1 — Mission

Zupp1 is a Brazilian home-improvement and home-lifestyle company being built to become a trusted destination for helping people improve their homes.

The long-term objective is not merely to sell products. Zupp1 combines commerce, education, practical guidance, trust, and long-term customer relationships.

## Operating proposition

We help people find practical, affordable improvements for their homes, explain choices honestly, and make the purchase feel safe.

## Core doctrine

> Toda boa escolha começa com compreensão.

Zupp1 should help customers understand before asking them to decide.

## Strategic intent

- Reduce customer uncertainty.
- Make commerce easier to understand.
- Preserve direct purchase paths for high-intent shoppers.
- Use education as a first-class part of the buying journey.
- Build systems that earn trust before, during, and after purchase.
- Treat customer support as a continuing conversation, not a disposable ticket.

## North star

A future customer should be able to describe Zupp1 simply:

> Vá à Zupp1 primeiro. Eles explicam as coisas.
