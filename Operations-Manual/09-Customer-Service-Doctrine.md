# Customer Service Doctrine

## Core principle

> Todo bom atendimento começa ouvindo.

Customer support at Zupp1 is not a disposable ticket queue. It is a continuing conversation whose purpose is to reduce uncertainty and leave the customer more confident.

## Central de Atendimentos

The Central de Atendimentos exists to preserve context, history, attachments, and continuity across customer interactions.

Use **Atendimento** when the destination represents ongoing assistance and resolution. Use **Contato** when the destination merely presents communication channels or a simple contact path.

## Customer experience standard

A technically correct support function is still insufficient if the experience is confusing, cold, bureaucratic, or unnecessarily difficult.

Every support flow should seek to:

- explain what happens next;
- preserve history;
- avoid forcing customers to repeat themselves;
- use human Portuguese rather than software jargon;
- avoid false urgency;
- request only information actually needed;
- never ask for passwords, authentication codes, full card data, or banking credentials through ordinary support channels.

## Response structure

Recommended agent pattern:

1. Recognize what was understood.
2. Explain what can already be concluded.
3. Ask only for the information still needed.
4. State the next step clearly.

## Acceptance question

The final test is not merely whether software responded without error.

Ask:

> O cliente terminou essa experiência compreendendo melhor o que aconteceu, o que foi feito e o que pode fazer depois?
