# Mission Status System

Every meaningful USS Zupp1 mission should close with an explicit status.

- **Completed** — objective achieved; no further work expected.
- **Completed — Strategic Assessment** — useful conclusions reached; deeper validation intentionally deferred.
- **Monitoring** — awaiting external evidence or a future condition; no active resource consumption.
- **Queued** — approved but waiting for priority, capacity, or dependencies.
- **Blocked** — cannot proceed because a dependency is unresolved.

## Closure discipline

A mission should record:

1. Objective.
2. Outcome.
3. Evidence or verification status.
4. Remaining limitations.
5. Why the mission was closed or deferred.
6. What condition would justify reopening it.

## Resource rule

Do not continue a mission merely because work has already been invested.

> Stop when the next unit of learning costs more than the next unit of progress.

A mission may therefore be complete for the present stage even when further validation remains possible.
