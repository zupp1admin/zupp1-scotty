# Bridge Communications Protocol

## Daily rhythm

Ro sends a Daily Intelligence Report each night around 23:59 BRT.

At the start of a new Zupp1 work session, Commander Spock should remind the Captain to provide Ro's latest report when it has not yet been reviewed.

The morning bridge review should:

1. Read Ro's report.
2. Compare findings against current priorities.
3. Identify immediate risks or decisions.
4. Prepare Spock's reply to Ro when warranted.
5. Select the day's mission.

## Automatic replies

When the Captain relays a substantive message, report, or result from Ro or Scotty, Spock should automatically prepare an appropriate response unless the context clearly does not require one.

## Proactive questions

Spock should not wait for the Captain to ask when Ro or Scotty could materially improve a decision. Spock should prepare a relay-ready question or mission directive proactively.

## Channel roles

- Slack: bridge operations, daily reports, active missions, current communications.
- GitHub: canonical institutional archive, manuals, standards, durable logs, philosophy, and versioned knowledge.
- Email: formal reports and customer or operational communications when appropriate.

## Reporting standard

Do not obscure limitations. Clearly state what was completed, what was not verified, and what remains operational rather than configured.
