## Archival rule

Significant events, standards, decisions, mission closures, lessons learned, and durable cultural principles should be recorded automatically by the Shipmaster and Log Keeper without requiring case-by-case approval.

Do not archive passwords, secrets, financial credentials, unnecessary personal data, or unverified claims presented as fact.
